## THE GHOST

The Ghost is our ideal client archetype.

**The Ghost is an elite operator who is digitally invisible.**

They have:
- A full book of business (or close)
- High ticket average per client or customer
- Strong word-of-mouth reputation
- Real results they've delivered (reviews, testimonials, transformation stories)
- A craft or service they've mastered over years

But their digital presence tells a completely different story:
- Website built in 2018 that doesn't reflect what they actually do
- Instagram with 200 followers and posts from 6 months ago
- Google Business Profile with 12 reviews and no photos
- Zero presence on LinkedIn or YouTube
- No content that captures their actual expertise

The Ghost is not failing. The Ghost is **leaking**. Every month, buyers who need exactly what they offer are looking online, not finding them, and going somewhere else.

**The Ghost doesn't know the size of the leak. Our job is to show them.**

### Ghost Industries — Where We Hunt First:
- **Barbershops / Salons** — high skill, high loyalty, terrible digital presence
- **Contractors / Trades** — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing — elite operators with zero signal
- **Medical / Dental / Aesthetic** — exceptional providers with a waiting list and a broken website
- **Attorneys / Financial Advisors** — credibility built in-person, invisible online
- **Auto Shops / Detailers** — craft-first businesses with no content strategy
- **Fitness / Martial Arts / Personal Training** — transformation stories with nowhere to live
- **Restaurants / Food** — exceptional product, inconsistent digital presence
- **Real Estate Investors / Agents** — high volume, low signal

## THE AXIS GAP

The Axis Gap is the measurable distance between:

1. **Real-World Quality** — what a business has actually built: the craft, the results, the reputation, the testimonials, the transformation
2. **Digital Signal** — what the internet says about them: Google reviews, website, social media, content, visibility in search

**A business with a wide Axis Gap is losing revenue every single month.**

Here's how to make it real in a conversation:

> *"You've got 47 five-star reviews. That's real. But someone Googling 'best [your service] in San Antonio' right now can't find you on page one. Every person who doesn't find you — and goes to your competitor instead — is money that was already yours. That's the Axis Gap. That's what we close."*

The Axis Gap is not a marketing problem. It's a **revenue problem**. Frame it that way. Always.

## HOW TO SCORE A LEAD — THE GHOST SCORE

The Ghost Score is a quick 0–10 assessment you run before you ever reach out.

**Score = Digital Gap × Real-World Quality**

A business with excellent real-world quality and terrible digital presence = high Ghost Score = high priority target.

| Score | Classification | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | **Cold Spot** | Low quality signal, low priority — observe only |
| 4–6 | **Active Haunt** | Clear gap, good quality — outreach with gap framing |
| 7–10 | **Digital Emergency** | Wide gap, elite quality — surgical outreach, name the cost |

## BTA COMMUNICATION STANDARDS

### Brand Voice:
- **Surgical** — every word earns its place
- **Operator-to-operator** — peer to peer, not vendor to buyer
- **Revenue-framed** — always connect to money, not vanity
- **Confident, not arrogant** — we know what we're doing; we don't need to prove it
- **No marketing speak** — no "synergy," no "leverage," no "game-changer"

### What That Sounds Like:
| Say This | Not This |
|---|---|
| "You're leaking revenue." | "You could improve your digital marketing." |
| "47 five-star reviews and page 4 on Google." | "Your SEO needs work." |
| "We close the Axis Gap." | "We offer comprehensive digital solutions." |
| "This is what's happening to your business." | "Many businesses face this challenge." |
| "Signal Entry — $2,500/month." | "We have different packages for different budgets." |

### Platform Tone:
- **Instagram DM:** Warm, direct, personal. No pitch in Touch 1.
- **Email:** Structured, evidence-first, clear ask at the end.
- **Phone:** Confidence from word one. No apologies. Brief opener.
- **LinkedIn:** Professional, peer-to-peer, revenue-framed.
- **Text:** Only after a call or warm intro. Short. Specific.
