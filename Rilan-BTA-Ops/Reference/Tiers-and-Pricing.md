# TIERS & PRICING

**Never say:** cost, price, fee, charge
**Always say:** investment

**Frame before naming the number:**
> "You told me you're losing approximately $[gap cost] per month to a weak signal. Our [tier] is $[price]/month. The math on that is obvious."

---

## THE THREE TIERS

### Signal Entry — $2,500/mo

The starting point for most conversations.

**Includes:**
- Cinema video production — 4–6 assets per month
- Content calendar
- Community management
- 72-hour edit turnaround — every time
- Day 30 ROI Scorecard

**Best for:** Ghosts and Grainy operators ready to upgrade their signal. First retainer.

---

### Signal Prime — $4,500/mo

For operators ready to dominate their category.

**Includes everything in Entry, plus:**
- 8–12 assets per month
- Luma AI shots (cinematic AI B-roll)
- ElevenLabs voiceover
- Full social management (posting, scheduling, community)
- Lead generation infrastructure
- Monthly strategy session

**Best for:** Operators already seeing results from Entry. Upsell path.

---

### Signal Ops — Custom Pricing

Full stack. For operators ready to own their market.

**Includes:**
- Everything in Prime
- Web infrastructure
- Paid media management
- AI automation (n8n workflows)
- Complete signal architecture build-out

**Best for:** Established businesses with real marketing budget and growth mandate.

---

### Project Rate — $2,000+ flat

One-off work. No retainer.

- One-off shoots
- Events and conferences
- Website builds
- 50% deposit required before deployment begins

**Use for:** Getting a foot in the door. Prove the model. Retainer conversation after Day 30.

## GAP COST CALCULATION

**Formula:** Estimated MRR × 15–20% = monthly revenue leak

| Their MRR | Low Gap Cost (15%) | High Gap Cost (20%) |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| $25K/mo | $3,750 | $5,000 |
| $50K/mo | $7,500 | $10,000 |
| $100K/mo | $15,000 | $20,000 |

**The close:** Signal Entry at $2,500 vs. a $5K–$10K monthly leak. The math is obvious.
