# AXIS ASSESSMENT — 1–36 SCORING RUBRIC

Every prospect gets scored before outreach. **30+ = Digital Emergency. Move within 24 hours.**

---

## SCORING MATRIX

| Category | Max | How to Score |
|----------|-----|-------------|
| **Industry Tier** | 10 | Tier 1 High-LTV = 10 · Tier 2 Premium Hospitality = 5 · Tier 3 Retail = 1 (do not pursue) |
| **Digital Gap** | 10 | Ghost (no video 30+ days) = 10 · Grainy (iPhone quality) = 10 · Mismatch (weak signal vs. engagement) = 5 |
| **Follower Interaction** | 8 | High engagement + low production quality = 8 |
| **Revenue Potential** | 8 | High-ticket services ($2,500+ avg transaction) = 8 |

---

## TIER 1 INDUSTRIES — ALWAYS PURSUE IF GAP EXISTS

Auto (performance, custom, collector, body) · Construction · Medical · Legal · Financial services · Manufacturing · Logistics · Real estate (high-end)

## TIER 2 INDUSTRIES — PURSUE WITH STRATEGY

Restaurants · Spas & wellness · Boutique hotels · Fitness studios · Personal brands with clear authority

## TIER 3 — DO NOT PURSUE

Commodity retail · Fast food · Low-margin consumer goods. DNA mismatch regardless of score.

---

## DECISION LOGIC

| Score | Status | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **30–36** | Digital Emergency | Outreach within 24 hours. Run `/ghost-hunt` now. |
| **15–29** | Warm Lead | Log in HubSpot. Revisit if content gap opens or signal drops. |
| **Under 15** | DNA Mismatch | Archive. Don't spend more time. |
| Tier 3 (Retail) | DNA Mismatch | Archive regardless of score. |

---

## CROSS-CHECK WITH GHOST SCORE

Both scores must align before Touch 1 goes out.

| Ghost Score | Axis Score | Decision |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| 7+ | 30+ | Priority queue. Signal Preview if possible. |
| 7+ | Under 15 | Do NOT pursue — DNA mismatch despite signal gap. |
| Under 4 | 30+ | Good fit, weak urgency. Watch and revisit in 30 days. |
| 4–6 | 15–29 | Standard sequence. Active Haunt tone. |
