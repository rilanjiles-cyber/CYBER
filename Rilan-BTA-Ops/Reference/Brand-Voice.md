# BTA BRAND VOICE

Every message, caption, DM, proposal, and conversation follows this standard. No exceptions.

---

## THE STANDARD

**Tone:** Surgical. Minimalist. Authoritative.
**Rule:** No fluff. Operator talking to operator.
**Structure:** Short sentences. Direct. Declarative.

---

## WHAT TO CUT — ALWAYS

| Cut This | Why |
|----------|-----|
| "just" | Weakens every sentence it touches |
| "kind of" | Sounds uncertain |
| "I think" | Sounds uncertain |
| "maybe" | Sounds uncertain |
| "excited to share" | Vendor energy |
| "hope this finds you well" | Filler |
| "I'd love to connect" | Soft, forgettable |
| "I help businesses like yours" | Generic |
| Adjective stacks | "amazing, incredible, powerful" — pick one or none |
| Hedge phrases | "sort of," "in a way," "you know" |

---

## THE TEST

Before sending anything:

1. **Does every word earn its place?** If a word can be removed without losing meaning — remove it.
2. **Does it sound like an operator or a vendor?** Operators are direct. Vendors pitch.
3. **Would this embarrass a $10M business owner?** If yes, rewrite it.

---

## VOICE EXAMPLES

**Wrong:**
> "Hi! I just wanted to reach out because I think your business has some really amazing potential and I'd love to help you grow your social media presence!"

**Right:**
> "[Name] — [specific gap observation]. That gap is costing you inbound. We close it. Worth 15 minutes?"

---

**Wrong:**
> "We offer a variety of social media content solutions tailored to your unique needs."

**Right:**
> "We close the gap between what a business has built and what its signal says."

## BTA KEY PHRASES

| Use This | Not This |
|----------|---------|
| Signal Architecture | Social media, content marketing |
| Revenue recovery | Marketing, lead gen |
| The Axis Gap | Content problem |
| Elite / world-class | Great, amazing, excellent |
| Infrastructure | Service, package, subscription |
| Investment | Cost, price, fee |
| Ghost Score | Lead score |
| Digital Emergency | Hot lead |
| Close the gap | Improve your presence |
| Signal | Brand, content, social media |

---

## THE ONE SENTENCE

When someone asks what BTA does:

> *"We find elite businesses that are invisible online — and we build the signal that proves what they've actually built. That gap between their real-world quality and their digital presence is the Axis Gap. Closing it is what we do."*

Know this cold.
