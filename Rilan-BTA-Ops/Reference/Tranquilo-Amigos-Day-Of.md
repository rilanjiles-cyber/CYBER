## YOUR ROLE — 4 THINGS

**1. Open the room (first 60 seconds)**
You know one of these owners from high school. Use it. Catch up like people, not salespeople. You set the temperature — Devin closes from it.

**2. Ask Q7 (yours to own)**
> *"What does Tranquilo Amigos look like in 2 years if everything goes the way you want it to?"*

If they mention expanding or shipping — that's Devin's cue for the 46-state angle. Don't skip this question.

**3. Watch the room during the pitch**
Devin is talking. You're watching. One owner drifting? Re-engage. Energy drops? Pull the personal thread back. Someone nodding hard? Affirm it.

**4. Handle the third owner objection (if it comes up)**
> *"Look, I've known [name] since high school — I'm not gonna waste your time or his. What would he need to see to feel good about this?"*

Goal: walk out with *"we're in if [name] signs off"* — not *"we'll think about it."*

Three things. Know them:

|---|---|
| **SSL warning on their website** | Browsers flag it as unsafe. Kills trust the second someone from Dallas clicks an ad. National shipping dies before it starts. |

## WHAT WE'RE SELLING (Simple Version)

**Step 1 — Fix the broke stuff:** SSL, Instagram consolidation, establish the visual look.

**Step 2 — Build the content machine:** 4+ cinematic reels a month. The *iced look* — high contrast, tight framing, luxury streetwear feel. The *audio/visual mismatch* — video shows something random, the voiceover teaches them about a product (terpene profiles, effects, etc.). Stops the scroll. Can't be banned by platform rules.

**Step 3 — Make customers come back:** Loyalty tiers (Amigo → Tranquilo → Bloque → La Cima). Referral system. Automated reorder reminders. Gamified return mechanics. Monthly La Cima spotlight on Instagram.
