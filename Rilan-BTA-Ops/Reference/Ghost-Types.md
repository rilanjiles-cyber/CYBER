# GHOST TYPES — WHO WE FIND

Not every business is a BTA client. We find one specific type — and two variations.

---

## THE GHOST

> **A world-class business that the internet doesn't know exists.**

- No professional video content in 30+ days
- Online presence doesn't reflect real-world quality
- Excellent offline reputation, invisible online
- Usually sustained by word-of-mouth — but capped by it

**Examples:**
- Custom race car builder doing six-figure builds with an iPhone photo account
- Law firm dominating their market for 20 years with no video presence
- Barbershop with a 5-star reputation and 200 followers
- Medical practice sustained by referrals — Google presence looks like 2009

**Ghost Score signal:** Content Frequency = 0, Platform Consistency = 0

---

## THE GRAINY

> **They post, but the quality doesn't match the standard.**

- Regular content output — but iPhone-only, inconsistent, no production value
- The gap is quality, not frequency
- Business looks mid-tier online despite being a premium operator

**Examples:**
- $5M construction company with shaky job site videos
- High-end restaurant posting blurry food photos
- Boutique fitness studio with gym-selfie-level content

**Ghost Score signal:** Production Quality = 0

---

## THE MISMATCH

> **High engagement, weak signal. Audience is there — production isn't.**

- Real following, real engagement — but the content looks amateur
- The audience has already found them; the content isn't converting it
- A production upgrade closes the gap fast

**Ghost Score signal:** Follower Interaction = 8 (high), Production Quality = 0–1

---

## THE AXIS GAP

The distance between what a business has built in the real world and what its digital signal says about it.

> Every Ghost, Grainy, and Mismatch is losing 15–20% of potential revenue to that gap. Every day the signal stays broken is a day they've already paid for.

**Our frame:** Revenue recovery. Not marketing. Not content. Revenue recovery.
