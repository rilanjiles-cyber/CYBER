# Skill — AI Knowledge

**Area:** Technology / AI Tools
**Why it matters:** AI is the leverage multiplier. The operators who understand how to use it are doing the work of 5 people. The ones who don't are competing against those 5 people.

---

## THE CORE CONCEPT

AI knowledge isn't about understanding the math. It's about knowing:
1. **What each tool can and can't do**
2. **How to give it the right input to get useful output**
3. **Where to plug it into your workflow so it saves real time**

The skill is prompting + workflow design, not engineering.

## THE TOOLS I'M BUILDING ON

### Claude (Anthropic)
**What it is:** The most capable AI assistant for complex reasoning, writing, and analysis.
**How BTA uses it:** Signal Scout, Touch drafts, Axis Assessments, call prep, case studies, objection handling.
**The key:** The Master Context Block. Load the BTA worldview once → every output stays on-brand.
**My current level:** Developing — getting better at prompt specificity.

**Prompting principles I'm learning:**
- Role + Context + Task + Format + Constraints = strong output
- Specific beats generic every time — give it real data, not vague descriptions
- Constraints clean up output faster than corrections
- One task at a time — don't ask for five things in one prompt

## WHAT I'M LEARNING ABOUT AI IN GENERAL

### Prompting is the skill
The AI is only as good as the instructions it gets. The difference between a generic output and a useful one is almost always in how the prompt was written.

**Good prompt = Role + specific context + exact task + format + what NOT to do**

### AI as a leverage tool, not a replacement
AI doesn't replace judgment. It accelerates execution.
- Claude doesn't decide who to reach out to — Rilan + Devin do.
- Claude doesn't approve messages — Devin does.
- Claude makes the research and drafting 10x faster so the human judgment can be applied to 10x more prospects.

### The workflows that matter most for BTA right now
1. Signal Scout → Ghost Score in under 5 minutes per prospect
2. Touch draft → ready for Devin review in under 3 minutes
3. Call prep → full brief in under 5 minutes before any discovery call
