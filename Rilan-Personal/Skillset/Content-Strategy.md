# Skill — Content Strategy

**Area:** Content / Signal Architecture
**Why it matters:** Content strategy is how you turn attention into revenue. The difference between content that converts and content that just exists is a system — not more posts.

---

## THE CORE CONCEPT

Content strategy is not "what should we post." It's answering three questions:
1. **Who** are we trying to move, and from where to where?
2. **What** do they need to see to trust us enough to act?
3. **How** do we build a system that delivers that consistently?

At BTA: the answer to all three is Signal Architecture.

---

## THE BTA CONTENT FRAMEWORK

### The Three Content Jobs
Every piece of content does one of three jobs:

| Job | Purpose | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Stop** | Interrupt the scroll | High-contrast visual, pattern break, unexpected hook |
| **Stay** | Give them a reason not to swipe | Education, entertainment, transformation proof |
| **Act** | Move them toward a decision | CTA, offer, booking, inquiry |

Most businesses only post "Stay" content. The Ghost has no "Stop." The Grainy has no "Act."

### The Visual/Audio Mismatch Format
- **Video:** Unrelated, odd, or unexpected visual
- **Audio:** Product education — terpene profile, service walkthrough, skill demonstration
- People stop because it looks different. People save because it's useful.
- Survives platform restrictions for regulated industries (hemp, finance, medical)

### The Iced Aesthetic
- High contrast, cold color grade — pushed hard
- Deep blacks, cold highlights, muted midtones
- Tight framing — voyeuristic, like the camera is right up on the subject
- Feels like luxury streetwear, not a neighborhood shop
- This is what stops the scroll AND lives on the website as one visual system

## WHAT I'M LEARNING

### Platform Mechanics
- **Instagram Reels:** Hook in 0–2 seconds. Vertical. Audio-on assumption.
- **LinkedIn:** Text-first. Story > tips. Professional credibility.
- **YouTube Shorts:** Retention rate > everything. Cut fast.
- **TikTok:** Trend-adjacent. Authenticity wins over polish here.

### Content System vs. Content Output
The mistake: thinking more posts = better results.
The truth: a system that produces 4 high-quality assets/month outperforms 30 random posts.

**A content system has:**
- A defined visual language (the "iced" look)
- A content calendar with purpose for each post
- A shooting cadence (batch production — not one-off)
- A distribution plan across platforms
- A feedback loop (what performs, what doesn't, why)

### Signal-to-Revenue Path
Every piece of content needs an answer to: "what does someone do after watching this?"
- No CTA = no path = no revenue
- Best CTA: one specific action, tied to highest-value service, low friction
