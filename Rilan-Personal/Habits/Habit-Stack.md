# RILAN'S HABIT STACK
## The Daily Standard — Starting 2026-05-28

> Habits aren't motivation. They're infrastructure. You build them once and they run.

---

## THE MORNING BLOCK

**Non-negotiable. Before the phone, before the noise.**

- [ ] **No phone for first 15 minutes** — own the first thought of the day
- [ ] **Hydrate immediately** — water before coffee
- [ ] **Set one intention** — one sentence: what is today for?
- [ ] **Movement or workout** — 6 days/week (lifting + running, lean-out phase)

*Why it matters:* The first 30 minutes of the day sets the cognitive tone for everything after. If you start reactive (phone first), you stay reactive.

---

## THE WORK BLOCK — BTA DAILY STANDARD

- [ ] 10 prospects scored
- [ ] 5 Touch 1 drafts → tagged DRAFT — DEVIN REVIEW
- [ ] 3 follow-ups checked
- [ ] 1 decision pushed
- [ ] Text Devin: "X leads in, X drafts tagged"

*This is non-negotiable on sprint days. No zero days.*

---

## THE LEARNING BLOCK

**15–30 minutes daily. One thing, done well.**

- [ ] **Read or watch something deliberate** — sales, content, AI, investing, music
- [ ] **Log it** — open a Skill Note. Even 3 sentences. The habit is the log, not the length.

*Compound knowledge works the same as compound interest. 15 minutes a day = 90 hours a year.*

---

## THE EVENING CLOSE

**Before bed. 10 minutes max.**

- [ ] **Review the day** — did I do what I said I would?
- [ ] **One win** — name something that went right
- [ ] **One thing to carry forward** — what matters tomorrow?
- [ ] **Phone down 30 min before sleep** — non-negotiable for recovery

## THE WEEKLY RHYTHM

**Sunday evening (15 min):**
- [ ] Review the week — did I run the stack?
- [ ] Set the 3 priorities for next week
- [ ] Check open loops — what moved, what's stuck?
- [ ] Write a weekly reflection (`Reflection/` folder)

**Monthly (first Sunday of each month, 30 min):**
- [ ] Monthly reflection — score all 5 categories
- [ ] Review 3 Directions — are they still right?
- [ ] Update Skill Notes — where have I moved?
- [ ] Adjust the habit stack if needed

---

## HABIT TRACKING — HOW TO LOG

Use the daily note (`Cmd+Shift+I`) — the Habit template is pre-loaded.

Check boxes as you go. Don't backfill. If you missed something, mark it missed and move on. The score over time matters more than any single day.

**The metric that matters:** How many days did I run the full stack this week?
- 7/7 = elite
- 5–6/7 = strong
- 3–4/7 = needs attention
- Under 3/7 = diagnose what's getting in the way

---

## THE RULE

**No zero days.**

A zero day isn't a missed workout or a missed morning routine. A zero day is a day where you did nothing that moved any of your directions forward. Those are the days that cost you.

Even 10 minutes of deliberate work beats a zero.
