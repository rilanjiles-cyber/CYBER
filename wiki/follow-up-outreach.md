## THE 3-TOUCH RULE

Every prospect gets exactly **three touches** before the sequence closes.

No more. No fewer.

Three touches is enough to: open the loop, deliver value, and close cleanly. More than three crosses into desperation. Fewer than three leaves money on the table.

**The sequence is over when:**
- They respond (positive, negative, or neutral) → enter active conversation flow
- All three touches are sent and 14+ days have passed with no response → move to "Cold — Reassess in 60 days"
- They say no explicitly → log, tag, mark closed. Respect it.

**Nothing goes out without Devin's review.**

---

## THE 3-TOUCH STRUCTURE

| Touch | Day | Purpose | Length | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Touch 1** | Day 1 | Open the loop. Name what you noticed. Ask one question. | Max 5 sentences | Curious / Direct / Surgical (match Ghost Score) |
| **Touch 2** | Day 3–5 | Deliver value. Advance the idea. Soft ask. | Max 5–6 sentences | Operator to operator — give them something real |
| **Touch 3** | Day 10–14 | Close the loop. Name the cost one last time. Leave the door open. | Max 3–4 sentences | Confident, respectful, final |

---

## TOUCH 1 — OPEN THE LOOP

**Purpose:** Get them aware. Not to close — to open a conversation.

**Rules:**
- Name what you found (specific data point)
- Ask one question that surfaces their awareness of the gap
- Do NOT mention BTA services, tiers, or pricing
- Do NOT use the word "marketing"
- Peer-to-peer — not vendor-to-buyer

**Touch 1 Tone by Ghost Score:**

| Score | Tone | First Sentence Energy |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Curious | "I came across your place and noticed something..." |
| 4–6 | Direct | "I pulled your Google presence and found something specific..." |
| 7–10 | Surgical | "You've got [X] five-star reviews and you're ranking page [X] for [keyword]. That gap is costing you." |

**What Touch 1 is NOT:**
- An introduction to BTA
- A pitch deck
- A request for a call (unless Ghost Score is 9–10)
- Generic ("I help businesses like yours with their digital presence")

---

## TOUCH 2 — DELIVER VALUE

**Purpose:** Give them something real. Advance the idea. Move them closer to a decision.

**Value options for Touch 2:**

| Value Type | What It Is | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Signal Preview** | 3-bullet brief of exactly what you found — their gap, in plain language | Ghost Score 6+ — show your work |
| **Proof Point** | One specific result from a similar client — industry, result, timeline | Ghost Score 4–7 — make it real |
| **Industry Insight** | A relevant data point they may not know ("70% of clicks go to page 1 results...") | Cold Spots and lighter Active Haunts |
| **Follow-up Question** | If they responded with soft interest — ask the question that moves them to a call | When they're already in conversation |

**What Touch 2 is NOT:**
- A copy of Touch 1
- Generic follow-up ("Just checking in...")
- A full pitch deck
- A list of your services

**Touch 2 Template Framework:**

```
[Value drop — lead with the insight or proof]
[Connect it to their specific situation — one line]
[Soft ask — "15 minutes?" or "Can I send you what I found?"]
```

---

## TOUCH 3 — CLOSE THE LOOP

**Purpose:** Final contact. Clean close. Leave on good terms.

**Two versions:**

**Version A — Neutral Close** (Ghost Score 0–6):
Leave the door open without naming the cost again.

> *"Last reach-out from me on this — didn't want to leave it open-ended. If the timing ever shifts, I'll be here. The gap I found isn't going anywhere."*

**Version B — Cost Anchor Close** (Ghost Score 7–10):
Name the cost one final time. Make it stick.

> *"Last message from me. Based on what I found, this gap is running you somewhere around [$X/month]. If and when you're ready to close it, reach out — this is recoverable. Rilan, BTA."*

**What Touch 3 is NOT:**
- Desperate ("Please just give me 5 minutes...")
- Passive-aggressive ("I guess you're not interested...")
- A pitch (still no services/pricing)
- Longer than 4 sentences

## RESPONSE HANDLING — WHEN THEY REPLY

When a prospect responds to any touch, the standard sequence pauses. You enter **active conversation mode**.

### Response Type → Next Action

| Response | What They're Saying | Your Move |
|---|---|---|
| **"Interesting — tell me more"** | Soft yes — curious, not committed | Send Signal Preview (if not sent) + offer 15-minute call |
| **"What is this exactly?"** | Needs framing — not sure who you are or what BTA does | One-paragraph explanation of BTA, revenue recovery frame, then offer a call |
| **"Send me something"** | Not ready to talk — wants to evaluate on their own | Send a clean, brief Signal Preview + one strong proof point + call offer with specific date |
| **"Not interested"** | Soft no — often premature | Acknowledge, don't push. "Got it — if it ever becomes relevant, you know where to find me." Log, close, reassess in 90 days. |
| **"How much does it cost?"** | Skipping straight to price — high intent | Don't lead with price. "Depends on what we're closing. Can I give you a 10-minute breakdown first?" → get to a call |
| **"Call me"** | High intent — move immediately | Call within 5 minutes. Use the 5-Beat Call Structure from `sops/bta-sales-system.md`. |
| **"I have someone already"** | Defense — not a hard no | "Good — who are you using for [specific gap area]?" → surface the specific gap their current person isn't closing |

## SEQUENCE TIMING — QUICK REFERENCE

| Ghost Score | T1 | T2 | T3 | Total Window |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 (Cold Spot) | Day 1 | Skip (unless they respond) | Skip | Day 1 only |
| 4–6 (Active Haunt) | Day 1 | Day 4 | Day 12 | ~2 weeks |
| 7–10 (Digital Emergency) | Day 1 | Day 3 | Day 8 | ~10 days |

**Digital Emergencies move faster.** The gap is wide, the cost is real, and the urgency is justified. Don't drag it out.

## TRACKING IN HUBSPOT

Every contact in an active sequence should have:
- [ ] Ghost Score in the Ghost Score field
- [ ] Current touch status in the pipeline stage
- [ ] Next action date set (use HubSpot Tasks)
- [ ] All messages logged in Activity
- [ ] Notes on any response and what was said

**Pipeline stage names for reference:**
- Prospect Identified → Touch 1 Queued → Touch 1 Sent → Touch 2 Queued → Touch 2 Sent → Touch 3 Queued → Touch 3 Sent → Engaged → Call Scheduled

---

## SEQUENCE QUALITY STANDARDS

A completed 3-touch sequence is done right when:
✅ Each touch is distinctly different (not rephrasing the same message)
✅ Each touch escalates slightly in specificity or urgency
✅ No touch mentions BTA services until Touch 2 (and even then, only if it fits)
✅ Price is never mentioned until a call is scheduled
✅ Every touch was reviewed by Devin before sending
✅ All touches are logged in HubSpot with timestamps
✅ Sequence outcome is recorded: Converted / No Response / Negative Response
